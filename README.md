# HexSoftwares_Sales-Dashboard-Analysis-in-Excel-And-PowerBI_(Interactive Dashboard Created Using Power BI)

# Project Objective:
The goal is to transform raw sales data into an interactive dashboard that helps
businesses monitor performance, identify top-selling products, compare regional
sales, and make data-driven decisions.

## Dataset Used
[Dataset](https://github.com/Joyce3346/Clothing-Sales-Dashboard/blob/main/Project%20one%20(shopping_trends).xlsx)

## KPIs
The dashboard highlights key business metrics at the top for quick decision-making:
•	Total Sales: $233K
•	Total Customers: 3.90K
•	Average Order Value: $25.35
•	Average Rating: 3.75

[Dashboard link](http://github.com/Joyce3346/Clothing-Sales-Dashboard/blob/main/Screenshot%202026-07-26%20192432.png)

## Customer Demographic Analysis
Sales by Age Category
Breaks down revenue across different customer age groups.
Shows that the Elderly (45+) segment contributes the highest share of sales.
Enables businesses to understand which age groups generate the most revenue.

## Sales by Gender
Compares sales contributions between male and female customers.
Male customers account for a larger portion of total sales than female customers.

## Business Value
Track overall sales performance.
Understand customer demographics and buying behavior.
Identify top-selling products and categories.
Monitor regional sales trends.
Make data-driven marketing, inventory, and sales decisions.
Improve customer targeting and revenue growth strategies.


## Conclusion
This project is a Power BI analytics project that visualizes retail sales data through interactive KPIs, demographic segmentation, product performance metrics, and geographic sales analysis. The dashboard enables stakeholders to uncover customer purchasing patterns, evaluate category performance, and make informed business decisions using dynamic filters and intuitive visualizations.
